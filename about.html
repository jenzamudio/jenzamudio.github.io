RESEARCH INTERESTS

Understanding people. Designing better systems.

My research sits at the intersection of human factors, industrial and organizational psychology, healthcare, and measurement science.

I am particularly interested in how human connection shapes people's experiences of healthcare and work, and how organizations and systems can be designed to support meaningful connection and wellbeing.

RESEARCH VISION
How can we design healthcare and work systems around the human experiences that matter most?

My work begins by understanding people's experiences, develops rigorous ways to measure those experiences, and ultimately aims to translate those insights into better-designed systems.

BACKGROUND

From psychology to engineering and back to people.

My academic path began in psychology and has expanded into engineering, allowing me to approach human problems from both behavioral and systems perspectives.

I am interested in research that does more than describe human experiences. I want to understand those experiences well enough to design environments, technologies, and organizational systems that help people thrive.
