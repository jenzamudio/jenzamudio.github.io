RESEARCH VISION
Designing systems that don’t lose sight of the people moving through them.

My research sits at the intersection of human factors engineering, industrial and organizational psychology, and measurement science. I am particularly interested in how human connection shapes people's experiences of healthcare and work, how we can measure these interactions, and how organizations and systems can be designed to support meaningful connection and wellbeing.
